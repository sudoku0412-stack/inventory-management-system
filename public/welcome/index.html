Everything in one place

  • Expiry and low-stock alerts

    See what is expired, expiring soon or running low, and get reminders before it matters.

  • Several shops, one account

    Run more than one shop, switch between them, and see an overview and printable report across all of them.

  • Made for your kind of stock

    Pick a shop type with the right forms, units and storage locations, or create your own.

  • Fast to fill in

    Scan barcodes with your camera, snap a pack photo to suggest the name and expiry, or import a CSV.

  • Work together

    Invite members to keep the stock up to date, and see a history of every item added, used or discarded.

  • Works on every device

    Use it in the browser or install it like an app. It keeps working when the connection drops.
