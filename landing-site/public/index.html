FAQ

Questions, answered.

Who can sign in?

Access is by invitation. A shop owner adds your email address, and you sign in with a one-time code sent to it. If you do not have an invitation yet, ask the owner of the shop.

What can I keep track of?

Anything you count: medicines, groceries, supplies, parts. Each shop has its own forms, units and storage places, and you can add your own.

Will it remind me before things expire?

Yes. You get a weekly summary email on Mondays and expiry alerts on your device, and the dashboard always shows what is expired, expiring soon or running low.

How does scanning work?

Point your camera at a barcode to look the product up, or at the expiry date to fill it in. The date is read on your device and nothing is uploaded. You always confirm the details before saving.

Can the whole family or team use it?

Yes. Owners invite members to each shop. Everyone sees the same stock, and the history shows who added, used or discarded an item.

Does it work without a connection?

Mostly. You can install it like an app, and changes you make offline are saved and sent when you are back online. Photos need a connection.

Who can see my stock?

Only the people invited to that shop. Sign-in is protected by Cloudflare Access, and each shop’s items stay inside that shop.
